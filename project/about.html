About

Japan is a fascinating mix of ancient tradition and modern innovation. It is located in East Asia, this island nation is made up of four main islands: Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku and over 6,000 smaller ones!

History

Japan’s culture is influenced from Shinto and Buddhist traditions. Today, the country blends historical landmarks like Kyoto’s temples and Nara’s deer parks with futuristic technology and cities.

Getting There

Most travelers arrive through major airports like Tokyo’s Narita or Haneda, or Osaka’s Kansai International. Their public transportation is world-famous especially the high-speed bullet train Shinkansen which makes getting around easy and fun.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March-May): Cherry blossoms and mild weather Autumn (September-November): Beautiful red and orange foliage. Winter: Snow festivals in Hokkaido and ski resorts. Summer: Exciting festivals and fireworks
